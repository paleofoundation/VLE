## Implemented in the actual production source

- Private structured sourcing briefs; optional contaminant targets; certification, quantity, destination and timing requirements.
- Separate private remediation notes and progress; explicit sharing of the supplier-facing summary; unsharing and closing; downloadable private dossier.
- Documented lot candidate comparison with evidence gaps, never invented passing values.
- Cross-account conversations, messages, expiring quotes, sample requests, mutually agreed cost responsibility, shipment references, buyer evaluation and expiring soft holds.
- Private PDF submission and owner review. A reviewed report can accompany a response and is available to that conversation's buyer account.
- Durable in-app notifications and Resend delivery with idempotency, preference checks, retry backoff and team-scoped recipients.
- Buyer/supplier team accounts, exact-email invitations, explicit domain joining, viewer/editor/admin/owner roles, revocation, account switching and stale-account write protection.
- Karen (karen@paleofoundation.com) owns the Kasandrinos team; Tony (tony@kasandrinos.com) is its founding administrator. No domain is enabled by default. A verified domain never proves company authority on its own; the account owner must explicitly allow it.
- Existing Kasandrinos storefront editing honors team permissions. Catalog evidence and prices retain their separate access gates.
- Public footer, privacy, about and contact routes.

## Verification

`node tests/sourcing.mjs`: isolated real D1/R2 checks of confidentiality, sharing, cross-account conversations, transitions, stale versions, report permissions, email retries and preferences. Email transport is mocked; no test messages go to real users.

`node tests/teams.mjs`: ownership, roles, exact domains, revoked access, account isolation and storefront permission.

Existing storefront and Kasandrinos tests, TypeScript check and production build. Browser visual checks use isolated test snapshots for authenticated layouts and the local real public routes; fixtures are removed before packaging.

## Boundaries and outstanding external work

Live TECRID credential validation, laboratory ordering and authenticated numerical result extraction remain external integration work. Current PDFs do not establish target compliance. Supplier stock, quotes and confirmation of results must come from the supplier.

Email credentials already exist in production. New email notification transport is tested against a mock provider, not a live recipient inbox. Immediate delivery and retries run after sourcing API requests through Worker `waitUntil`. There is no configured unattended timer; a queued failure retries on a subsequent sourcing request after its backoff. Do not describe this as a scheduled retry service.

Soft holds are non-binding and expire. Orders, payment processing, freight booking and finished-product certification are outside this release. Supplier PDF review does not automatically publish new public catalog listings.

## Deployment

Sites applies append-only D1 migrations during publication. Preserve the existing public audience and R2/D1 bindings. The old local repository is retained untouched. Use the Sites source repository for future changes; do not deploy the old Next.js checkout over the current domain.
