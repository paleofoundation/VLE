# Native TECRID consumer

Paired with TECRID mint step 4 draft #38. Receiver remains OFF. This change is a separate draft above main, which already contains proof exchange #64 and proof chips #75. No merge, publication, deployment, live migration, secret provisioning or external message is authorized by this document.

The original provisional consumer rejected all thirteen exact synthetic metadata/tombstone fixtures from the tested producer. The new consumer accepts native middle-dot TECRIDs/lifecycle IDs and the agreed `vle-evidence/1` stream. It requires the exact recipient, digest of `(TECRID, recipient)`, monotonic sequence, signed TECRID supplier identity, reviewed mapping ID and VLE supplier identity. Lot matching remains exact and unique; unmatched/ambiguous or changed supplier/lot identities stay withheld.

Only `analytical-record` is currently supported for native credentials, displayed as Lab result, not a certification. A real certification requires a separately verified contract and is never inferred from a laboratory result. Upserts require current verification attestations, an active unexpired grant, a matching scoped snapshot and its SHA-256. Status-only snapshots cannot contain findings, selected analytes cannot exceed their declared scope, and narrower scopes cannot contain full signed/canonical content. Raw-body HMAC is verified before parsing or storage, within the existing 64 KiB bound and five-minute signature window.

Migration 0012 adds schema only: per-credential/recipient stream state; a unique stream/sequence receipt; and independent grant rows with nullable private payloads. It does not backfill or promote provisional `lot_tecrids` records. Those old rows are retained for review and do not enter the native public projection. A received grant's controller and lot identity cannot change. Independent grants are separate even when their TECRID matches. A replacement explicitly points to its predecessor within the same controller/recipient scope and permanently removes the predecessor payload; revoking the replacement does not restore old access.

Receipt, stream watermark and grant mutations commit together. Competing duplicates produce one receipt; reused event/receipt/sequence identities with different content are rejected. Higher-sequence version/fingerprint regressions are rejected. Older unseen events can be acknowledged as stale but cannot change data. A tombstone may arrive before its original upsert or overtake a failed delivery; the retained watermark prevents delayed restoration. Tombstones do not claim current evidence verification and contain no result, snapshot, canonical or signed payload. Grant withdrawal clears only that grant. Credential revocation clears every grant payload in its stream. Minimal receipt and grant metadata remains for audit.

Private snapshots have no public read endpoint. Public organization/lot proof uses an explicit metadata allowlist, current stream version, active unexpired grant and explicit public consent. Brand inheritance still requires the existing confirmed release plus that grant's exact downstream VLE organization scope. Deduplication happens after those filters; it cannot combine grants into broader access. The existing evidence-bound lot-identity guard also covers native grants.

Verification includes exact producer fixtures (copied byte-for-byte from TECRID #38), local real-D1 migrations, raw HMAC, per-grant withdrawal, payload removal, tombstone-first delivery, delayed replay, replacement persistence, concurrent receipts, controller isolation, current-version checks, default/private consent and rejected scope/mapping/recipient/environment input. Existing proof, registry, role, release, public rendering, Worker, security and dependency tests remain required. All accounts and payloads used in tests are synthetic; provider calls are intercepted.

Local cross-tests are not a live-site rehearsal or a physical phone SLA. Deployment still requires the full TECRID mint/security chain, #33 fix propagation, operator-reviewed mappings and consent, distinct credentials/private storage, both reviewed draft migrations and the separate disposable-account rehearsal. Existing downloaded copies and requests already in flight cannot be recalled. Channel outage/retry conditions remain operational gates.
